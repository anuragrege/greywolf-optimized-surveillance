Fitness Function:
The fitness function measures the proximity of the drone to the disaster area.
Fitness = 1 / √((x2 - x1)² + (y2 - y1)² + 0.0001)
where (x1, y1) are the drone coordinates, and (x2, y2) are the disaster center coordinates.
